    answer: "The average rent in Spain is €1,184/month according to the Observatorio del Alquiler (2025).     answer: "Madrid asking rents average €22.7/m² (Idealista, December 2025) and €21.17/m² (Fotocasa, February 2026). Barcelona reaches €23.8/m² (Idealista, December 2025), the highest of any major Spanish city. Both cities sit roughly 50–60% above the national average."

  - question: "What is the gross rental yield in Spain in 2026?"
    answer: "The gross rental yield in Spain reached 7.02% in December 2025, according to the Banco de España — a new record high, up approximately 1 percentage point versus 2024. This reflects rent growth outpacing house price growth in most markets."
  - question: "What percentage of their salary do young people spend on rent in Spain?"

As a result, the youth emancipation rate fell to 15.2% in the second half of 2025 — a historic low. Only 1 in 7 young Spaniards lives independently of the parental home."
